02 / YOUR DAYS AT LAMAEL CHALTÉN / PATAGONIA

The mountain is
only half the day.

The other half begins
when you come back.

You come to El Chaltén to walk. But the hours between the trails deserve a place of their own: a room in warm light, a table to return to, time with your boots off.

You’ve walked all day.
Now, take your time.

Leave your boots by the door and your bag beside the bed. Timber, linen and warm afternoon light: this is the part of El Chaltén you can enjoy sitting down. Tomorrow’s walk can wait.

03 / STAY A LITTLE LONGER

Tomorrow begins
at the table.

A little to share as you talk over the walk. Bread by the window the next morning. At LAMA, there’s room in the trip for sitting together as well as setting out.

Make LAMA part of your days in El Chaltén.

03

EL REFUGIO

THE HOUSE, NOW
THE ROOM / AT LAMA

Take the boots off.
Stay a while.

Timber, linen, light across the bed. After a day on foot, leave your bag where it lands and take a little time to be still. There’s no need to fill every hour.

The light changes.
You don’t have to follow it.

THE RETURN

A familiar door
at the end of the walk.

By the second evening, you recognise the way back. The timber, the door, the room where you left your things. In a trip shaped by going further, there’s pleasure in returning to the same place.

04 / LA COCINATHE TABLE

Let the walk
become a story.

A plate between you. Something to share while the details come back: where you stopped, what you saw, the stretch that felt longer on the way down.

At the table, there’s time to tell it properly.

No miles left
to count tonight.

THE NEXT MORNING

Before the laces,
the table.

There will be time for the trail. First, bread by the window and a few minutes to decide what kind of day you want.

Let the morning belong to the stay, too.

ANOTHER DAY IN EL CHALTÉN

Out you go.
With somewhere to return to.

The room, the table, the walk. Let that become the rhythm of your time in El Chaltén. You can spend the day looking ahead and still look forward to coming back.

YOUR TIME AT LAMA

Come for the walking.
Stay for the rest.

Make the room, the table and the next morning part of your days in El Chaltén.
