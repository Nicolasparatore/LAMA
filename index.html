LAMA   /   LA HISTORIAA FAMILY’S RECOLLECTIONS
SOME THINGS RETURN WHEN WE PAUSE
1986   /   THE ARRIVAL

We came for
the distance.

Our parents drove south with two children and enough money for a few months. They wanted to live near the mountains.

They rented a house. When winter came, they stayed.

1993   /   THE SPARE ROOM

The first guest
was waiting out the rain.

A walker missed the last bus. Our mother made up the spare bed. He stayed three nights, then helped repair the gate.

That is how we remember it beginning.

1998   /   THE LONGER TABLE

One more place.
Then one more.

People arrived with a name written on a piece of paper. Some left at dawn. Others put it off another day.

We bought more chairs before we thought to put up a sign.

TODAY   /   THE SAME PLACE

A hostería,
almost by accident.

There were permits, a second bathroom, years of repairs. Somewhere in there, the house became LAMA.

We still live here. We still check the weather before setting the table.

People leave. A little of them stays.
